# RAIL Learning Videos: Practitioner Guide

This document outlines the video curriculum for mastering the RAIL framework in the Energy sector.

## Module 1: The Myth of the Static Model
- **Duration**: 5:00
- **Key Concept**: Why "Historical Accuracy" isn't enough for offshore operations.
- **Visuals**: A graph showing model accuracy dropping as sensor drift increases.
- **Takeaway**: Static models are "blind" to the present.

## Module 2: Implementing Adaptive Feedback
- **Duration**: 8:30
- **Key Concept**: How to capture technician feedback as "Realtime Grounding Data".
- **Visuals**: Code walkthrough of the RAIL feedback loop implementation.
- **Takeaway**: Human-in-the-loop is a feature, not a bug.

## Module 3: RAIL vs. Traditional - The Performance Delta
- **Duration**: 6:15
- **Key Concept**: Measuring the cost of downtime avoided by RAIL.
- **Visuals**: Side-by-side comparison of error rates during a simulated "Day Zero" event.
- **Takeaway**: RAIL ROI is measured in responsiveness.
