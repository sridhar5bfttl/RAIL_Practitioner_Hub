## 🌪️ The Challenge: Offshore Dynamic Volatility
Offshore rigs and energy plants operate in "Constant Flux." Traditional AI models rely on historical snapshots, leading to the **Static Gap**:
- **Sensor Drift:** Gradual environmental changes that pre-trained models ignore.
- **Novel Faults:** New equipment behaviors that weren't in the training set.
- **Extreme Latency:** Waiting for model retraining while assets are at risk.

## 🚀 The RAIL Solution
The RAIL framework bridges the Static Gap through **Adaptive Grounding** and **Continuous Alignment**.
